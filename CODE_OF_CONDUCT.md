# Code of conduct

Participants must communicate respectfully, discuss the work rather than the person, and avoid
harassment, threats, discrimination, unwanted sexual attention, disclosure of private information,
and deliberate disruption. Technical disagreement is welcome when it is specific and evidence based.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may limit
participation when necessary to protect contributors or the project. Reports should include links or
screenshots only when needed and must omit private data. Good-faith reports will be handled as
privately as the available GitHub tools allow.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces
managed by the project. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately when
appropriate, and open to reconsideration when new evidence appears.
